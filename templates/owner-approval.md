# Owner Approval Checklist — TEMPLATE (BLANK)

All items must be "yes" before any publication. A single "no" means
no publication.

- [ ] The finding was reported privately to the owner first.
- [ ] The agreed remediation window has ended, or the owner agreed
      in writing to earlier publication.
- [ ] The owner confirmed the fix, or confirmed that publication may
      proceed without a fix.
- [ ] The owner approved the exact publication text, including the
      level of technical detail.
- [ ] All evidence in the publication is redacted: no secrets,
      tokens, credentials, personal data, or internal addresses.
- [ ] No exploit detail is included for any issue that is still
      unfixed in a deployed version.
- [ ] The publication states the scope honestly: what was tested,
      what was not, and whether the finding is a lab exercise or a
      real disclosure.
